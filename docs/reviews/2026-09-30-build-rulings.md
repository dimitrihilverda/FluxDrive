# Rulings made while building v1 (plan tasks 1–12)

The plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-29-fluxdrive-v1-hardware.md` was carried out task by task. Where the plan
was silent, contradicted the spec or could not work as written, the executor decided and wrote the decision down
as a ruling: what was decided, why, and what it costs if it was wrong. This file keeps all of them, in the order
they were made, together with the minor findings that were deferred. The reviews themselves, with every finding
and its decision, are the other files in this folder.

61 rulings, 9 deferred minor findings.
